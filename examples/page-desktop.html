Warehouses live

Built on an append-only ledger

No stored quantity to drift out of sync. Ask what the stock was at any instant and the answer is a replay, not a guess.

◆

Event-sourced counts

Receipts, picks, adjustments and counts are facts. The quantity is a fold over them, recomputed on demand and cached, never written by hand.

◇

Reservations that hold

Availability subtracts open reservations at read time, so two orders can never take the last unit — even across regions.

○

Cycle counts, not shutdowns

Count a shelf while the warehouse keeps running. Discrepancies land as their own events with an author and a reason.

From dock to dispatch

Four steps, each one an event on the same ledger.

Receive

Scan the pallet. Quantities land against a purchase order and a location.

Reserve

An order claims units the moment it is placed, before anything is picked.

Pick

The reservation converts to a movement, routed by shortest travel path.

Dispatch

Stock leaves the ledger and the carrier handoff is recorded with it.
