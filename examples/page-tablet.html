Questions we get asked

Does it replace our WMS?

No. It owns the count and the ledger; your WMS keeps routing and labour. The event stream is the seam between them.

What happens to historic adjustments?

They import as events dated when they happened, so opening balances replay correctly rather than appearing as a lump on day one.

Which scanners are supported?

Anything that emits keyboard input, plus native integrations for the three common Android rugged handhelds.
